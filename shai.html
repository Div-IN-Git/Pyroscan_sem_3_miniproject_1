01 — Background

The Growing Crisis
of Forest Fires

Forest fires cause large-scale ecological damage, biodiversity loss, and significant economic impact across India.

Many regions globally face recurring fires due to dry vegetation, rising temperatures, and human activity.

Current monitoring systems are reactive rather than predictive — fires are detected after they've already begun.

End Users

Forest Departments Disaster Management Environmental Agencies
02 — Problem Statement

Reactive Systems Are
Not Enough

Forest fire management currently relies on post-ignition detection and manual risk assessment. There is no unified system that combines satellite imagery, environmental factors, and historical fire data to proactively identify high-risk forest zones.

04 — Proposed Solution

PyroSCAN : How It Works

PyroSCAN processes multi-source satellite and environmental data from Sentinel-2 and MODIS.

AI models analyze vegetation stress, temperature, terrain, and proximity to human activity.

Outputs dynamic forest fire risk maps classified as Low, Medium, and High risk.

End result: Proactive monitoring instead of reactive response.

05 — Novelty & Show Stopper

What Sets Us Apart

06 — Methodology

Data to Decision Pipeline

Satellite APIs
Raw Earth Data
QGIS API
PyroPredict

Satellite APIs provide raw Earth observation data, geospatially processed using QGIS for spatial alignment and accuracy. The cleaned data is passed into an ML model analyzing historical and environmental patterns. Outputs are presented through an interactive dashboard.
